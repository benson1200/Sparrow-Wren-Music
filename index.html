Story

Sparrow Wren began as a handful of songs traded between friends after midnight—simple melodies on acoustic guitars, lyrics scribbled in the margins of old notebooks, and a shared sense that music could still feel honest and human.

Over time, those sketches grew into a full band: layered harmonies, dynamic drums, warm keys, and electric lines that lift the folk roots into something bigger.

Sparrow Wren plays to connection more than spectacle—inviting listeners into the narrative, whether it’s a full stage, a small listening room, or a stripped-down house show.
